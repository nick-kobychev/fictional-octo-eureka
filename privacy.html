Household budget: privacy policy

This is a private, non-commercial budgeting application used by one household to read its own bank accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data used

With the account holder's consent, the application reads account details, balances and transactions from the household's own accounts through Enable Banking.

Purpose

The data is used only for the household's own budgeting.

Storage and sharing

The data is stored on a device in the household's home network. It is not sold or shared, apart from the exchange with Enable Banking and the account provider that is needed to retrieve it.

Withdrawing consent

The account holder can withdraw consent through their bank or by removing the link in the application, and can delete the stored data from the application at any time.
